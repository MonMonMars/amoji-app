Vocal bursts in public/voice-clips/{yue,zh,ja,en}/{female,male}/
laugh, smile, giggle, wow, um, oh, ha.

Greetings (hello, nice, hihi, pleasure) and the longer loading lines
(think, search) are still the espeak clips. They are not in this list.
apps/web/scripts/gen-voice-clips.py does not overwrite the bursts.

Clips are trimmed, faded, and encoded to mono MP3. Ideal length is under 3s.
Freesound takes were cut from the CC0 or CC BY high-quality preview of the
same recording, because the original wav needs a logged-in download.
Nox, VocalSound, and Mixkit takes were cut from the original wavs.
Pixabay was not used. OpenGameArt group giggling was not used: it is a
crowd, not a solo companion. 効果音ラボ, NonSpeech7k, Expresso, and
NonverbalTTS were not used.
